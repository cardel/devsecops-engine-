# Reports of the original campaign

Reports of four cases of the original campaign, as the engine produced them:
Juice Shop, FitFusion Backend, SecureTaskAPI and SimpleHealthAPI. The workflow
artifacts of WebGoat, DVWA and PyGoat had expired and were not preserved.

Each folder contains `findings.json` (normalized unique findings),
`gate_decision.json` (the three gate decisions and the F1 metrics),
`ai_evaluation.json`, `scan_config.json`, `recon_context.json` and
`SECURITY_REPORT.md`.
